What Avalon is

A creed for those who would live chivalry.

Avalon is a belief system and creed founded on the revival of chivalry. It is a demanding path of discipline, strength, honor, and service: a knightly way of being in the modern world.

It does not require belief in a deity, nor does it deny one.

It asks only that those who walk it do so with seriousness and integrity.

Avalon holds a creed, and we call it the Way: the Code, the Virtues, and the Tenets that set out how one who follows Avalon lives.

Why “Avalon”?

In the old legends, Avalon was the resting place of Arthur,

and the place from which it was said chivalry would one day return.

We take that name as our own.

Not as nostalgia, but as a declaration:

that chivalry is needed now more than ever.

Know before you read on

Avalon is not woke. Avalon stands firmly against most tenets of left-leaning ideology. We say so plainly, on the front page, so you can decide right away whether this is for you.

A creed and a calling

A creed to live. A religion to those who hold it as one.

Avalon holds a creed, the Way, and lives it as a way of life. For some Avalon is their religion; for others the Way is a calling to follow. Avalon is registered as a religion and a church so that the Way can be followed, and developed, freely. Avalon does not make a deity of anything, nor does it deny one, and it sits easily beside many faiths. What you do matters more to the Way than what you profess.

The three pillars

Train the body, the mind, and the spirit.

These three pillars of being must be made and kept strong in order for one to stay on the Way through the difficulties of life. Martial arts are the core of chivalry: they forge mind, body, and spirit into one keen edge, and the weapon arts stand at the heart of Avalon’s culture. Not for aggression, but for the defense of the innocent and the effective service of the Way.

Train the Body

Practical physical prowess, kept strong through daily training.

Train the Mind

A lifelong student: study, research, create, and apply what you learn.

Train the Spirit

Meditation, and contemplation of the Virtues and the Way.

The Order of Avalon

For those who would live the Way in full.

Anyone can choose to follow the Way. The Order of Avalon is something more: a Martial Chivalric Order and Brotherhood founded on Avalon and the Way, which trains to a standard and puts that training to work to empower, enlighten, and protect. Men and women alike, held to the same standards.

Common questions

Know what you are answering.

Why is Avalon considered a religion?

Avalon holds a creed, the Way, and lives it as a way of life. For some Avalon is their religion; for others the Way is a calling to follow. Avalon is registered as a religion and a church so that the Way can be followed, and developed, freely. Read the full answer.

What is the Creed of Avalon?

It is the Way. Avalon holds a creed: the Code, the Virtues, and the Tenets, which set out what one who follows Avalon does and does not do. We call that creed the Way. Avalon is the religion and church that holds and keeps it; the Way is what you follow. Walk the Way.

Do I have to be religious to follow the Way?

No. Avalon is registered as a religion and a church, and for some who follow it, it is their religion; others follow it as a creed and a calling. Avalon does not require belief in a deity, nor does it deny one, and you may keep your own faith so long as it does not interfere with the Way. Some beliefs are incompatible with the Way. What you do matters more to the Way than what you profess.

Can anyone follow Avalon?

Of course, anyone can choose to follow the Way. This, however, is not the same as joining the Order of Avalon. That being said, there are religions, beliefs, and ideologies that are incompatible with Avalon. About the Order.

Is Avalon a historical reenactment group?

No. Avalon is not a reenactment or living-history group. It is about bringing chivalry and its ideals into the present, not pretending to live in the past.

Though you will see symbolism, icons, ceremonies, and martial training reminiscent of the past, we use these traditions to keep the spirit of chivalry alive and present in the modern day.
