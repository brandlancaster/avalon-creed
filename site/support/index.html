The Order of Avalon

Avalon and the Order

Avalon is the body that holds the belief. Its creed, the Way, made up of the Code, the Virtues, and the Tenets, is published openly on this site.

Anyone can choose to follow the Way. That is not the same as joining the Order of Avalon. The Order is a Martial Chivalric Order: a committed Brotherhood of men and women, held to the same standards, that trains to a standard and puts that training to work to empower, enlighten, and protect.

The first line of the Code makes the difference clear. “To Serve and Defend Avalon, the Order, its Members, and its Missions” is, in the Code’s own words, specific to members of the Order of Avalon: a pledge to serve and protect.

The Order is in its founding phase and is not yet open for membership. Those who answer now will help build it.
