Frequently Asked Questions

Religion, who can follow the Way, reenactment, politics, and the Order.

Why is Avalon considered a religion?

Avalon holds a creed, the Way, and lives it as a way of life. For some Avalon is their religion; for others the Way is a calling to follow. Avalon is registered as a religion and a church so that the Way can be followed, and developed, freely. Read the full answer.

What is the Creed of Avalon?

It is the Way. Avalon holds a creed: the Code, the Virtues, and the Tenets, which set out what one who follows Avalon does and does not do. We call that creed the Way. Avalon is the religion and church that holds and keeps it; the Way is what you follow. Walk the Way.

Do I have to be religious to follow the Way?

No. Avalon is registered as a religion and a church, and for some who follow it, it is their religion; others follow it as a creed and a calling. Avalon does not require belief in a deity, nor does it deny one, and you may keep your own faith so long as it does not interfere with the Way. Some beliefs are incompatible with the Way. What you do matters more to the Way than what you profess.

Can anyone follow Avalon?

Of course, anyone can choose to follow the Way. This, however, is not the same as joining the Order of Avalon. That being said, there are religions, beliefs, and ideologies that are incompatible with Avalon. About the Order.

Is Avalon a historical reenactment group?

No. Avalon is not a reenactment or living-history group. It is about bringing chivalry and its ideals into the present, not pretending to live in the past.

Though you will see symbolism, icons, ceremonies, and martial training reminiscent of the past, we use these traditions to keep the spirit of chivalry alive and present in the modern day.

Where does Avalon stand politically?

Avalon stands for the freedom, nobility, virtue, and survival of humanity, regardless of the political currents. That being said, Avalon and the Order are not compatible with “woke” ideologies or many ideologies of the political left in Western cultures. Many of these ideologies are antithetical to human freedom, functionality, and survival. Read where Avalon stands.

Do men and women both walk the Way?

Yes. We use the word brotherhood without apology, and both men and women are welcome. In the Order of Avalon, everyone meets the same standards.

Does Avalon forbid drinking?

No. Sobriety is the ideal, and a clear mind is always the better state, but there is a time and place for drink. If you drink, avoid excess. One who is intoxicated is unfit for duty until sober, and drinking that becomes a problem will be addressed. Recreational drugs are not permitted. Read the Tenets.

Why do martial arts matter so much to Avalon?

Martial arts are the core of chivalry. They train the mind, the body, and the spirit, and forge the three into one keen edge. The weapon arts in particular stand at the heart of Avalon’s culture. Not for aggression, but for the defense of the innocent and the effective service of the Way.

What is the Order of Avalon?

The Order of Avalon is a Martial Chivalric Order founded on Avalon and the Way: a committed Brotherhood that trains to a standard and puts that training to work to empower, enlighten, and protect. It is in its founding phase. Read about the Order.

How can I support Avalon?

Give through GiveSendGo or Zelle, follow the Way, and share it with those who should hear it. Support Avalon.
