Positions

Where Avalon Stands

Current societal issues: Avalon’s positions, stated openly so you know where we stand before you walk the Way.

Plainly stated. Avalon is not woke and stands against most tenets of left-leaning ideology. We publish our positions openly so you know where Avalon stands before you walk the Way or seek to join the Order. They are stated with conviction and without contempt: Avalon judges ideas firmly and treats people with dignity.

Position 01

Sex and gender

Avalon affirms biological reality: there are two sexes, male and female. This is not a feeling or a spectrum.

We hold compassion for people who experience gender dysphoria. They deserve kindness, real support, and the truth. We oppose the medical transition of minors, and we hold that sports, prisons, shelters, and other single-sex spaces should remain sex-based for fairness, safety, and privacy. Adults are free to live as they choose.

Position 02

The family and children

Avalon holds the natural family, a mother and a father raising the children they bring into the world, as the strongest foundation for a healthy and virtuous society. We honor marriage, committed parenting, and the choice to raise children as among the highest callings a person can answer.

Single parents doing their best deserve compassion and practical support. Children are not the property of the state, schools, or movements: parents hold the primary right to raise and educate their own children.

Position 03

Responsibility and victimhood

Avalon calls every person to own their life. Hardship and injustice are real, but they need not define a future. Your past may explain you; it does not excuse you.

We oppose ideologies that teach whole groups they are permanent victims or permanent oppressors. Compassion and justice are both required, and true justice is rooted in personal responsibility. Every person is called to rise, to train, and to build.

Position 04

Race and identity politics

Avalon judges every person as an individual, never by race, skin color, or ancestry. We reject racism in every form, including systems that assign guilt, privilege, or different standards by skin color.

For that reason we oppose race-based quotas and the DEI and Critical Race Theory frameworks that divide people by group identity. We stand for equality under the law and in opportunity, for merit, and for unity through shared virtue.

Position 05

Immigration

Avalon supports legal, orderly, and assimilative immigration that strengthens the host nation. Nations have the right and the duty to control their borders and choose who enters.

Compassion for refugees and the genuinely persecuted belongs to chivalry, but compassion must walk with wisdom: immigration should be vetted, paced so that communities can integrate newcomers, and joined with the expectation that newcomers learn the language, respect the law, and contribute.

Position 06

Religion and religious law

Avalon judges every religion and ideology by one standard: does it uplift human flourishing, individual liberty, and virtue, or suppress them?

We have no quarrel with people of any faith who live peacefully, respect the law, and honor liberty and equality under the law. We oppose any system that would place religious law above civil law, subordinate women or people of other faiths, or punish apostasy, blasphemy, or speech, and that includes Sharia law. Honest examination of any belief system is not hatred.

Position 07

Men and women

Avalon honors the differences between men and women as complementary strengths, not defects to be erased. We support equal rights and equal opportunity for both, and standards that stay high and objective, never lowered to force equal outcomes.

We reject narratives that cast men as oppressors and women as victims. Healthy masculinity is protective, responsible, and strong; healthy femininity is graceful, nurturing, and resilient. Motherhood and fatherhood are noble callings, and those whose path differs face no judgment from us.

Position 08

Free speech

Avalon holds free speech as a non-negotiable right. Truth cannot be found, defended, or preserved without the freedom to speak it, even when it offends.

We oppose cancel culture and the use of “hate speech” rules to silence legitimate disagreement, while drawing the line at direct incitement to violence. True tolerance is the willingness to let others speak when you disagree with them.

Position 09

Abortion

Avalon regards human life as sacred from conception, and views the deliberate ending of that life as a grave moral wrong in the great majority of cases.

In the rare and tragic cases of rape, incest, or grave danger to the mother’s life, Avalon leaves the decision to the mother, her conscience, her family, and her faith. We oppose late-term abortion and abortion as birth control, and we call for real support for mothers, adoption, and a culture that honors parenthood. True chivalry protects the vulnerable: both the unborn child and the woman facing impossible circumstances.

Position 10

Stewardship of the Earth

Avalon believes in wise stewardship of the natural world: clean air and water, healthy land, and thriving wildlife. We acknowledge that the climate changes and that human activity contributes.

We reject alarmism and policies that sacrifice human prosperity and freedom, and we support practical solutions: nuclear and next-generation clean energy, conservation, reforestation, pollution control, and innovation that lifts people out of poverty.

Position 11

Sexuality and marriage

Every adult deserves dignity and the freedom to live their private life in peace, free from violence or persecution.

Avalon holds marriage between one man and one woman as sacred, and will perform marriages only between a man and a woman. We oppose the sexualization of children and the demand that public institutions celebrate any sexual ideology, and we defend the right of every person to live by conscience.

Position 12

Education and parental rights

Parents hold the primary right to direct the upbringing and education of their children. Schools should teach children how to think, not what to think, with strong foundations in reading, writing, mathematics, logic, and true history.

We support real educational choice: public, charter, private, religious, or homeschool. We oppose schools hiding information from parents or introducing sexual content and ideology to children.
