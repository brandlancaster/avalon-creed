The Creed

Creed of Avalon

Belief in Modern Chivalry as a Way of Life, Purpose, and Enlightenment.

Avalon is a belief system and creed founded on the revival of chivalry. It is a demanding path of discipline, strength, honor, and service: a chivalric way of being in the modern world.

It does not require belief in a deity, nor does it deny one.

It asks only that those who walk it do so with seriousness and integrity.

Why “Avalon”?

In the old legends, Avalon was the resting place of Arthur,

and the place from which it was said chivalry would one day return.

We take that name as our own.

Not as nostalgia, but as a declaration:

that chivalry is needed now more than ever.

The Way

To follow The Way is to live in accordance with the principles of chivalry.

It is to train the body, sharpen the mind, fortify the spirit,

and stand ready to protect what is good.

The path is difficult.

The standard is high.

The purpose is clear.

Why is Avalon considered a religion?

A creed to live. A religion to those who hold it as one.

Avalon is a creed and a way of life. For some it is their religion; for others it is a calling to follow. It is registered as a religion and a church so that the Way can be followed, and developed, freely.

Avalon is a belief system, a creed, and a way of life. For some who follow it, the Paladin among them, it is their religion. For others it is a vocation: a calling, a discipline, a Way to follow. Both are welcome, and the Way asks the same of each.

Why a religion and a church

Avalon is registered as a religion and a church to protect the right of every follower to walk the Way unhindered, and the freedom of Avalon itself to develop and keep the Way as it should be kept.

Your own faith

Avalon does not make a deity of anything, nor does it deny one. Members are free to hold their own religious beliefs, so long as they do not interfere with the Way. Avalon sits easily beside Christianity, Buddhism, and a number of other traditions, and it works as well on its own as it does alongside them. Some beliefs are incompatible with the Way.

A living creed

Avalon is concerned with what you choose to do with this life, whatever you believe comes after it. It is specific about the Way and about the responsibilities each of us carries in life.

A temple of training

Think of Avalon less as a sect and more as a temple tradition training in the spirit of the Knight: discipline of body, mind, and spirit, forged together, in the service of modern chivalry. The Creed, the Code, the Virtues, and the Tenets are published in full for anyone to read. In Avalon, what you do matters more to the Way than what you profess.

A note on the word “Knight.” The Avalon Creed addresses the one who walks the Way as a Knight. That is an ideal to strive toward, not a title. Following the Way does not make one a knight, and neither does membership in the Order of Avalon. In time the Order will have knights: knighthood is a pinnacle rank, earned only through exceptional service to the Order over many years.
