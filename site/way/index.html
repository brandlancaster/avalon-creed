The Way

Train the Body

Practical physical prowess, kept strong through daily training.

Train the Mind

A lifelong student: study, research, create, and apply what you learn.

Train the Spirit

Meditation, and contemplation of the Virtues and the Way.

It is to train the body, sharpen the mind, fortify the spirit, and stand ready to protect what is good. The Code sets the standard, the Virtues describe the character, and the Tenets set out the beliefs and daily practice.

A creed to live by

Avalon is a creed and a way of life. For some it is a religion, and it is registered as a religion and a church so that the Way may be followed, and developed, freely. For others it is a calling and a discipline. What you do matters more to the Way than what you profess.
