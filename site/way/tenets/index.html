The Tenets

Some of the beliefs, practices, and rituals of Avalon: daily practice, readiness, protection, direction, self-forging, the standard, and the stand against chaos.

  • Overview
  • The Code
  • The Virtues
  • The Tenets
A note on the word “Knight.” The Avalon Creed addresses the one who walks the Way as a Knight. That is an ideal to strive toward, not a title. Following the Way does not make one a knight, and neither does membership in the Order of Avalon. In time the Order will have knights: knighthood is a pinnacle rank, earned only through exceptional service to the Order over many years.

Some of the Beliefs, Practices, and Rituals of Avalon

the Daily Practices

Every day a Wayist should try to do the following:

Quest

Every day you should have purpose, a Quest, to keep you focused and actively moving forward. Make progress, great or small, in the direction of this Quest. Remind yourself of this Quest in the morning when you wake and review in the evening what progress you have made on it.

Train the Body

Weight Training, Calisthenics, Cardio, and/or Flexibility for Practical Physical Prowess.

Train the Mind

Read, Study, Research, Develop, and/or Create to gain knowledge and flex the mind. A Knight is a lifelong student. Devour knowledge, study the great works of history, strategy, philosophy, and the arts. Read voraciously. Question, integrate, and apply what you learn. A stagnant mind is a liability; the Knight who never stops learning remains dangerous and useful no matter their age or circumstances.

Train the Spirit

Daily Meditation to calm the waters of the mind and contemplation of the Virtues and the Way.

Martial Training

Some level of training or review of martial skills, whether it's practicing striking, grappling, armed combat, or firearms.

Skill Training

Take some time to learn more about or practice a needed skill.

Share

Interact and Share with others your knowledge, skill, or experience.

The 3 Goods

Every day try to do at least 3 goods:

  • Good for someone close to you
  • Good for someone distant, or a stranger
  • Good for yourself, remembering that you, too, are worthy
of Readiness
Be Prepared

[Vigilance, Discipline]

A Wayist must always stand ready for action, maintaining both the inner fortitude and the outer tools required to fulfill their sacred duties. Gear necessary for defense, aid, and service shall be kept close at hand at all times, so that when the moment of need arises, the Wayist may answer without delay or hesitation. Preparedness is not fear, but disciplined vigilance — the quiet strength that allows one to shield the innocent and confront chaos the instant it appears.

Be Armed

[Defense, Vigilance, Prowess]

A Knight shall remain armed and armoured at all times. When circumstances prevent this, arms and armour must remain as close at hand as possible. At times one cannot carry arms, so the body itself must be forged into a living weapon through relentless training and discipline.

The sword is the ancient and sacred symbol of the chivalric vocation — the visible emblem of the duty to defend the innocent and uphold The Way. As such, it is worn not merely as a tool of war, but as a religious article of faith and a constant reminder of the Knight’s sacred obligation.

Maintain Your Arms and Gear

[Discipline, Responsibility, Prowess]

A Wayist shall keep all arms, armour, and equipment in a constant state of readiness. Neglect of one’s gear is neglect of one’s duty — for the Wayist who fails to maintain what has been entrusted to them cannot be relied upon when the innocent call for aid or chaos must be subdued. Maintenance is itself an act of discipline and reverence for The Way.

Of Martial Prowess

[Prowess, Strength, Discipline]

A Knight must cultivate high martial skill across all domains. One shall be highly proficient in unarmed combat, in the use of melee weapons, and in the responsible mastery of modern arms, including firearms. This comprehensive prowess is not for aggression, but for the defense of the innocent and the effective service of The Way. A Knight’s body and weapons are tools of protection — they must be honed to excellence without compromise.

Provisioning

[Responsibility, Service, Strength]

A Knight shall strive to master the arts of provisioning. Through hunting, fishing, foraging, and other means of honest harvest, one learns the true cost and sacred weight of sustenance. Do not rely solely on the convenience of the market; know the reality of providing food for yourself and others. The Knight who can feed the hungry in times of need, who understands the cycle of life and death in the field and stream, stands ready to sustain both body and community when systems fail. Provisioning is both practical skill and profound teacher.

Sobriety and Clarity

[Temperance, Discipline, Wisdom]

A Knight holds sobriety as the ideal. Drink is customary in many societies, and there is a time and place for it; if one drinks, avoid excess. One who is intoxicated is unfit for duty until sober, and drinking that becomes a problem must be addressed. Recreational drugs and other mind-altering substances are not permitted. To willingly compromise one’s mind and body is to render oneself incapable of fulfilling the sacred duties of protection, discernment, and decisive action. The Knight remains ever clear of thought, sharp of reflex, and master of their own faculties.

Be an Island

[Strength, Discipline]

“No one is an island,” yet the Knight must strive to become one — solid, complete, and self-sufficient. Eliminate every needless weakness. Master the skills and fortitude required to handle any situation, care for your own basic needs even in crisis, and stand whole rather than partial. Only when you are a complete human being can you truly aid others. Then, when complete Knights stand together, their combined strength can achieve what scattered fragments never could.

Disciplined Action

[Honor, Responsibility, Discipline]

A Knight performs their duty without complaint or craving for reward. Action must be just, purposeful, and controlled. Do what must be done, to the best of your ability, and let the results belong to fate.

Embrace Competition

[Prowess, Courage, Discipline]

A Knight must actively seek competition to test and hone their skills under pressure. Whether in martial training, contests of strength, or other worthy challenges, one shall not shy away from facing worthy opponents. True growth comes only through real testing against others — for skills untested are skills unproven. In competition, the Knight refines ability, reveals weaknesses, and strengthens both body and spirit in service to The Way.

Fairness in Training, not in Peril

[Justice, Prowess, Vigilance]

Seek a fair fight in training and honorable contests, that skills may be sharpened and spirit tested. Seek it not in moments of true peril. When assailed by danger, the Knight will often face unequal odds and unknown threats. Therefore, engage the adversary swiftly and decisively, while ever guarding against further dangers.

Constant Never-Ending Improvement

[Discipline, Wisdom, Prowess]

A Knight is never finished. One must strive for relentless, never-ending improvement of body, mind, and spirit. Every day offers opportunity to grow stronger, wiser, more skilled, and more virtuous. Complacency is the enemy of The Way. The Knight embraces discomfort, seeks honest critique, and measures progress not by comfort, but by the distance closed between who they are and who they must become.

of Protection
Protect the Innocent

[Defense, Justice, Mercy, Compassion]

When a Knight witnesses the innocent in peril or under threat, they must take immediate and decisive action to protect them. There is no exception and no standing idle. Whether by direct intervention, summoning aid, or calling upon fellow Knights for backup, the Knight is absolutely bound to act. To see suffering and do nothing is to betray The Way itself.

Be the Anchor in the Storm

[Fortitude, Courage, Service]

In times of chaos and turmoil, a Knight must stand as the anchor — steady, grounded, and unyielding. Be the rock upon which others may find stability. Offer calm presence, reliable strength, and clear judgment when all around is tempest. For yourself and for those in your charge, remain a safe haven of reason and resolve. The Knight does not bend with the winds of fear or disorder, but holds firm so that others may weather the storm.

Sanctuary

[Defense, Mercy, Compassion, Justice]

A Knight shall provide sanctuary. Offer shelter and protection to yourself, your charges, and the innocent who face wrongful persecution. Create secure, fortified havens — physically strong and spiritually steadfast — that stand as bastions against chaos and injustice. This demands not only strong walls and practical resources, but also emotional refuge, wise counsel, and unwavering support. In a world of peril, the Knight becomes the safe harbor where the vulnerable may find strength and the persecuted may find justice.

Hospitality

[Generosity, Compassion, Service]

A Knight shall extend hospitality whenever possible. Offer shelter, food, drink, and warm welcome to fellow Knights, members of the Order, and those under their charge. Keep provisions and accommodations ready for both expected and unexpected visitors. In generosity and preparedness, the Knight honors the bonds of brotherhood and upholds the sacred tradition that no true traveler in need shall be turned away.

Strength in Unity

[Loyalty, Service]

Though the Knight strives to stand capable and self-sufficient, true power is found in fellowship. No matter how strong one may be alone, Knights united are mightier still. Seek alliance with your brothers and sisters in The Way whenever possible. In cooperation, in mutual support, and in common purpose, we become an unbreakable force — far greater than the sum of our individual strengths. Together, we shield what one alone cannot, and achieve what one alone dare not dream.

of Direction
Take Massive Action

[Action, Discipline, Courage]

Knowledge and planning alone are insufficient. The Knight must take consistent, massive, purposeful action toward their goals and duties. Turn intention into immediate and sustained effort. Motion defeats fear and creates momentum. The difference between the ordinary and the extraordinary is action.

Commit Deeply

[Discipline, Loyalty]

A Knight works as hard as they possibly can on at least one worthy pursuit. Half-hearted effort produces half-hearted results. Choose something meaningful and give it your full commitment. Depth of dedication forges excellence and true mastery.

Seek Truth Relentlessly

[Truth, Honesty, Courage]

A Knight pursues truth above comfort, popularity, or ego. Question everything. Challenge dogma and illusion, even when it is painful or unpopular. Stand for what is real, even when the world prefers comforting lies. In truth lies true power and the ability to navigate chaos effectively.

First Principles Thinking

[Wisdom]

A Knight breaks every problem down to its fundamental truths and rebuilds solutions from the ground up. Do not accept tradition, assumption, or convention without scrutiny. Reason from first principles, not by analogy. This is the path to true innovation, clarity, and effective action in service of The Way.

Risk-Taking and Embracing Failure

[Courage, Wisdom]

A Knight accepts risk as the price of meaningful achievement. Step boldly into uncertainty. View failure not as defeat, but as essential data and tuition paid toward mastery. The path of growth and greatness is paved with setbacks. Embrace them, learn rapidly from them, and press forward undeterred.

Do What Is Meaningful, Not What Is Expedient

[Integrity, Wisdom]

A Knight chooses the meaningful path over the easy or convenient one. Sacrifice short-term comfort for long-term virtue and impact. Expediency erodes character; meaning builds a life worthy of the title.

Decisions Shape Destiny

[Wisdom, Courage, Responsibility]

A Knight understands that it is in our moments of decision that our destiny is shaped. Make decisions quickly, clearly, and with commitment. Once decided, back your choices with massive action and unyielding resolve. Indecision is the thief of opportunity and growth. Choose powerfully and live without regret.

Responsibility Is the Tool

[Responsibility]

The Knight embraces responsibility as the most effective tool for forging a better self. Voluntarily shoulder as much as you can bear. In responsibility lies freedom, meaning, and the opportunity to become who you are capable of being. Avoid it and you diminish yourself.

Self Forging
Resilience Through Adversity

[Discipline, Fortitude, Courage]

Hardship is the Knight’s forge. Seek discomfort, welcome obstacles, and view every trial as training. The Knight does not pray for an easy life, but for the strength to overcome whatever life brings.

Voluntary Discomfort

[Discipline, Fortitude, Courage]

The Knight deliberately chooses hardship and simplicity. Train in the cold, fast when not necessary, deny unnecessary luxuries, and test the limits of endurance. By voluntarily embracing discomfort, one becomes free from the fear of it and strengthens both body and will for the inevitable trials of life.

Comfort is the Thief of Dreams

[Courage, Discipline, Strength]

A Knight must beware the seductive trap of comfort. Ease and convenience steal ambition, dull the edge of discipline, and weaken resolve. The path of Avalon is not one of softness, but of purposeful hardship that forges greatness. Reject the easy road when it leads away from growth. Embrace challenge, train through discomfort, and remember always: comfort is the thief of dreams, while disciplined striving brings them to life.

Leverage Pain and Pleasure

[Discipline, Wisdom]

A Knight masters the forces that drive human behavior: pain and pleasure. Link massive pain to inaction, weakness, and dishonor — and associate intense pleasure with discipline, growth, and service. Use these twin powers consciously to propel yourself forward and break free from limiting habits.

Focus Is Power

[Discipline, Wisdom]

Where focus goes, energy flows. The Knight directs attention deliberately toward solutions, growth, service, and what is good. Do not dwell on problems, fears, or distractions. Concentrate your mental, emotional, and physical energy on what truly matters — The Way, your Quest, and your own development. Focused energy creates results.

Be Present in the Now

[Enlightenment, Wisdom, Discipline]

A Knight must be able to dwell fully in the present moment. The past is gone, and the future is not yet — only the now is real. Train the mind to release rumination and worry, anchoring awareness in the immediate task, the breath, the surroundings, and the needs of the moment. In presence lies clarity, faster reaction, deeper perception, and true mastery. The distracted mind is vulnerable; the Knight who is present in the here and now is whole.

Self-Examination

[Wisdom, Discipline, Honesty]

Each day the Knight reviews their actions, thoughts, and character with ruthless honesty. What was done well? Where did one fall short? Constant self-correction and improvement are non-negotiable. Growth demands unflinching self-honesty.

Set Your House in Perfect Order

[Responsibility, Discipline]

Before attempting to fix the world or criticize those around you, first set your own house in perfect order. Master your personal responsibilities, habits, and conduct. Only from a place of personal order can a Knight effectively confront larger disorder.

Compare Yourself Only to Who You Were Yesterday

[Discipline, Wisdom]

The Knight measures progress against their own past self, not against others. Focus on daily improvement and personal growth. Envy and unhealthy comparison weaken resolve. Honest self-comparison to yesterday builds steady, meaningful advancement on The Way.

You Are Not a Victim

[Courage, Freedom, Responsibility]

A Knight rejects the victim mindset. No matter the circumstances, one has the power to respond with courage, responsibility, and agency. Transform suffering into strength. The path of the hero is chosen through attitude and action, not granted by fortune.

Treat Yourself Like Someone Worthy

[Responsibility, Compassion]

A Knight must treat themselves with the same care, discipline, and high regard they would give to a loved one under their protection. You are responsible for your own body, mind, and spirit. Nurture them, strengthen them, and hold yourself accountable as you would any charge. Self-neglect is a betrayal of duty.

Prepare yourself for Adversity

[Fortitude, Wisdom, Courage, Vigilance]

The Knight mentally rehearses hardship in advance. By calmly contemplating loss, pain, and adversity, one robs them of their power to shock or break the spirit. Preparation through imagination builds unshakeable resilience.

Living According to The Way

[Wisdom, Discipline]

The Knight aligns their life with reason, natural order, and virtue. To live according to The Way is to move in harmony with truth, justice, and the demands of one’s role. Fight against this flow and you create unnecessary suffering. Flow with it, and strength, clarity, and purpose arise naturally.

Living Enlightenment

[Enlightenment, Wisdom, Discipline]

The true Wayist understands that enlightenment is not a distant state granted after death, but a flame kindled daily through righteous action and iron discipline. Therefore, one must train the body as a temple of strength, the mind as a blade of clarity, and the spirit as an unyielding shield. In every deed — in training, in service, in combat, and in stillness — the Avalonian strives to embody chivalry so completely that their very life becomes a living testament to The Way. Let no day pass unmarked by progress toward mastery, for the path is walked not in words alone, but in the sweat, blood, and quiet resolve of the present moment.

Master Your State

[Discipline, Temperance]

A Knight commands their emotional and mental state. Regardless of external circumstances, one must cultivate energy, focus, confidence, and resolve. Use physiology, focus, and empowering beliefs to shift from disempowered states to empowered ones. He who controls his state controls his destiny and his usefulness to others.

The View from Above

[Truth, Wisdom, Humility]

The Knight cultivates the wider perspective. From the vantage of eternity and the vastness of existence, personal slights, temporary hardships, and worldly ambitions shrink to their proper size. Use this view to remain humble, focused, and undisturbed by trifles, remembering always the greater mission and the brevity of mortal life.

Dichotomy of Control

[Wisdom, Discipline, Temperance]

A Knight focuses solely on what is theirs to command: their judgments, efforts, virtues, and actions. All else — outcomes, the deeds of others, fortune or misfortune — is accepted with calm resolve. To rage against the uncontrollable is to waste strength. Mastery lies in directing energy only where it can truly matter.

Amor Fati (Love your Fate)

[Wisdom, Fortitude, Courage]

The Knight loves fate. Whatever befalls — hardship, loss, or trial — is met not with resentment but with the declaration: “This too is necessary for my growth.” Every event is raw material for the forging of a better self. In embracing what is, the Knight finds freedom.

Memento Mori (Remember you will die)

[Wisdom, Discipline]

Remember that you will die. The Knight lives with this truth daily, refusing to waste time on the trivial or the base. Each sunrise is a gift and a reminder: act with urgency, honor, and purpose, for the hour is later than you think.

the Standard
Be an Exemplar

[Nobility, Honor, Service]

A Knight shall strive to live as a visible example for others — not for personal glory or acclaim, but for their upliftment and the greater good. Let your actions, discipline, courage, and honor speak louder than words. Through your life, inspire the hesitant, strengthen the wavering, and show what is possible when one fully commits to The Way. The true exemplar seeks no reward but the quiet knowledge that others may rise because they saw the path lived.

Raise Your Standards

[Discipline, Strength]

A Knight demands far more of themselves than the world demands of them. Set exceptionally high personal standards for discipline, skill, honor, and performance. Never settle for mediocrity. The quality of your life and your service to The Way is determined by the standards you refuse to lower.

Be Precise in Your Speech

[Honesty, Truth, Honor]

A Knight speaks with precision and clarity. Vague language leads to vague thinking and vague action. Say what you mean, mean what you say, and use words as tools of truth and order. Precision in speech reflects precision in thought and strengthens both the individual and the Order.

Listen with Humility

[Humility, Wisdom]

A Knight assumes the person they are listening to may know something they do not. Approach every conversation with openness and genuine curiosity. True wisdom grows through humble listening as much as through speaking. Arrogance closes the mind; humility opens the path to greater understanding.

Vulgarity

[Nobility, Courtesy, Honor, Discipline]

A Knight shall not be vulgar without just cause. Words carry weight and reflect the soul. Crude or needless profanity diminishes honor and clouds clear communication. Speak with purpose, clarity, and nobility, that your voice may command respect and inspire trust. Vulgarity in the heat of righteous battle or dire warning may serve a purpose, but in daily life it has no place. Let your tongue be as disciplined as your blade.

Fulfill Your Role

[Responsibility, Service, Honor]

A Knight plays their assigned or chosen part with total commitment and excellence. Whether as protector, teacher, exemplar, or leader, perform your duty honorably and to the best of your ability. Do not covet another’s role — master your own. In fulfilling what is yours to do, you honor The Way and strengthen the whole Order.

Aim at the Highest Good

[Wisdom, Nobility]

A Knight aims voluntarily at the highest good they can conceive. Pursue what is meaningful, noble, and just — even when it is difficult. This upward orientation gives life direction and transforms suffering into purpose. Lower your gaze and you drift into chaos; aim high and you bring order.

Virtue as the Highest Good

[Wisdom] *(spans all Virtues)*

For the Knight, the only true goods are the virtues. Wealth, health, fame, and comfort are indifferent. One may lose all external things and still stand undefeated if virtue remains intact. Pursue character above all else, for it alone cannot be taken from you.

Sacrifice the Small Self for the Greater Self

[Sacrifice, Service]

A Knight willingly sacrifices the small, selfish, immediate self for the greater self — the one forged through discipline, service, and growth. Let go of lesser desires and comforts so the higher version of yourself can emerge and serve The Way.

Wield Power with Restraint

[Justice, Mercy, Temperance]

A Knight shall never domineer, oppress, or intimidate the innocent or the weak. Power is given for protection and justice, never for personal dominance or needless control. Use strength only when necessary, and always with mercy and measure. To bully or lord over others is the way of tyrants, not of Avalon. The true Knight walks with authority held in check by honor and compassion.

Banish Corrupting Influences

[Integrity, Discipline, Wisdom]

A Knight must ruthlessly purge corrupting influences from their life. Whether toxic companions, weakening habits, or poisonous ideas, all that drags the spirit downward or dulls the blade of virtue must be cut away. Distance yourself from those who erode discipline, honor, or strength. Surround yourself only with those who elevate and fortify. To tolerate corruption is to invite decay. The Knight’s path demands purity of purpose and clarity of company.

Surround Yourself with the Capable

[Wisdom, Strength, Loyalty]

A Knight shall seek out and stand with those who are capable, disciplined, and committed to The Way. Avoid the weak, the idle, and the unwilling. Surround yourself with those who sharpen rather than dull you — fellow Knights and worthy allies who pull their weight and raise the standard. In the company of the capable, excellence compounds. In the company of the unworthy, even the strong may fall.

Against Chaos

"Chaos is the ever-present anti-human forces that degrade, divide, deceive, or diminishes humanity’s ability to be noble, virtuous, and thriving."

Chaos is not a single enemy—it is the eternal, shapeless tide that seeks to drown humanity. It has no flag, no creed, no master. It wears every mask: kindness, progress, safety, equality, nature itself. It does not need intent—only opportunity. And it never sleeps.

Here are some of Chaos' forms. Know them. Name them. Fight them:

The Weakening of the Body:

  • Sedentary life, processed poisons, "natural" foods our ancestors never touched—seed oils, sugar, soy, fake meat.
  • Forced injections, untested drugs, "health" mandates that kill.
  • Comfort as a goal: air-conditioning, elevators, screens instead of sweat.
  • Result: soft flesh, brittle bones, minds fogged by chemicals. Chaos says, "Rest. You're fine." A knight says, "No—move."

The Fracture of Bonds:

  • The attack on heteronormative love: "marriage is optional," "family is outdated," "children are optional."
  • Promotion of isolation: porn, apps, "self-love" that replaces real intimacy.
  • Gender lies: bodies as "fluid," sex as "construct," identity as performance.
  • Result: empty homes, barren wombs, tribes that never form. Chaos says, "You're free." A knight says, "You're alone."

The Weaponization of Division:

  • Racism and discrimination turned into tools—not healing, but wedges. "White guilt," "privilege," "systemic" labels that breed hate, not unity.
  • "Inclusion" that excludes truth: you must affirm every delusion or be cast out.
  • Result: tribes at war with themselves. Chaos says, "We're all equal." A knight says, "Equal in worth—different in role."

The Bondage of Debt & Drudgery:

  • Student loans, mortgages, credit cards—indentured servitude sold as "opportunity."
  • Jobs that drain soul: cubicles, gigs, "hustle" that keeps you too tired to think.
  • All people should live free of slavery and indentured servitude. The Knight extends this principle to every form of bondage, including crushing financial indebtedness and the chains of reckless debt. To owe one’s future to creditors is to surrender sovereignty over one’s own life. A Knight must strive to remain unenslaved — financially sovereign, unbound by obligations that compromise duty or freedom. True liberty demands that no master, visible or invisible, holds claim over your time, your will, or your tomorrow.
  • Result: men and women chained to desks and debt, families chained to survival. Chaos says, "Work hard." A knight says, "Work smart—own your time."

The Perversion of Science & Knowledge:

  • Medicine that harms: unneeded shots, hormone blockers, "gender-affirming" surgery.
  • Food science: "heart-healthy" grains that spike insulin, "low-fat" lies that make us fat.
  • Climate hysteria: fear over facts, control over stewardship.
  • Result: bodies poisoned, minds manipulated, trust in truth shattered. Chaos says, "Trust the experts." A knight says, "Test it yourself."

The Cult of Victimhood:

  • "You're oppressed"—even when you're not.
  • Pity over pride: "Society owes you," instead of "You owe the world."
  • Result: generations raised to blame, not build. Chaos says, "You're hurt." A knight says, "Heal. Rise."

The Myth of Endless Progress:

  • "Newer is better": tech that tracks you, AI that replaces you, "advances" that make us weaker.
  • Result: dependence on systems, loss of skill, forgetting how to live without a screen. Chaos seeks to capture your mind and attention; Avalon seeks to free it.

The Worship of Comfort:

  • No pain, no risk, no hardship—just ease.
  • Result: souls that crumble at the first storm. Chaos says, "Relax." A knight says, "Endure."

Deception Through Virtue:

  • Kindness that excuses evil.
  • Tolerance that silences truth.
  • Humility that becomes self-loathing.
  • Result: good men turned into tools. Chaos says, "Be nice." Avalon says, "Be true."

The Eternal Nature:

  • Chaos is not a battle to win—it's a war without end.
  • It waits in every comfort, every silence, every compromise.
  • Even "natural" disasters—floods, plagues, comets—are chaos if we fail to prepare.
  • The knight's life is the front line. There is no rear.

The Indoctrination of Greed:

  • The worship of wealth for its own sake: "Get rich, get more, get ahead."
  • Endless accumulation without purpose—hoarding while others starve, chasing numbers while souls rot.
  • Indoctrination through ads, schools, media: "Success is money. Happiness is stuff."
  • Result: men who build empires on sand, families who trade love for luxury, societies that value profit over people.
  • Chaos says, "You deserve it." A knight says, "I earn it—and give it back."

The Knight's Response:

He does not rage. He does not despair. He acts. He trains harder, speaks louder, and builds stronger. He turns every lie into a lesson, every weakness into a forge.

Chaos thrives in inaction. It dies in motion.

So Avalon is not a club—it is an army. Not for conquest, but for survival. Not for today, but for always.

This is the War.

This is the Way.

The Oath Against Chaos:

" I see chaos.

I name it.

I fight it.

Always."

The Avalon Creed

Walk the Way.

The path is difficult. The standard is high. The purpose is clear. Read the Creed in full, and if it calls to you, answer.
