Avalon Code of Chivalry

(click each to expand)

To Serve and Defend Avalon, the Order, its Members, and its Missions+–

This line is specific to members of the Order of Avalon, a pledge to Serve and Protect.

To Live by the Code, the Virtues, and the Tenets of Chivalry+–

The agreement to be bound to the Ideals of Chivalry

This is a reminder, every time it is read or said, to revisit all of the ideals of Chivalry and reavow a dedication to them.

To Defend Humanity Against Chaos+–

To protect the people individually and as a whole against the cancerous and destructive influences and forces of Chaos.

Chaos takes many forms in today's world, some are obvious, yet many are deceptively masked, often as humanitarian or other beneficial works.

To Aid and Protect the Innocent+–

To help and protect those in need whenever possible.

Note, this does not say specifically the "weak", as feigned weakness is often used to attack others. The innocent are those without fault, those who are being attacked unjustly.

To Quest and to Champion Just Causes+–

Quests and Causes give purpose and focus to one's life. Quests should normally be specific, short-term missions with achievable goals to which one swears pursuit. Causes are generally longer-term movements and commitments that require group efforts.

Always have a quest, a purpose, driving you. Make it important, make it exciting, make it a reason to wake every morning.

To avoid Deception and to Never Betray+–

Outside of extreme need, one should not deceive. Betrayal is a violation of the human covenant and should never be done. This does not mean that you cannot disengage from an oath or loyalty, but it must be done and let be known well before you engage in a contrary path.

To avoid Wanton Damage or Offense+–

Though Warriors, followers of the Way should avoid damage or agression unless it is necessary.

To Train the Body, the Mind, and the Spirit+–

These three pillars of being must be made and kept strong in order for one to stay on the Way through the difficulties of life.

These pillars form the balance upon which you live. They must each be strengthened in order for you to reach as high as you can.

To Better the World, Yourself, and the Lives of Others+–

The Way teaches to be a force for good and to always strive for improvement.

To Obey the Law of the Land if it is Just+–

One should follow the Law of an area whenever possible.

To Promote and Teach the Way+–

It is an integral part of the Way to share the Way with others.
